1. Business Overview

Rise & Thrive Support Services, LLC is a nurturing residential group home for youth ages 10–17. We provide a safe place where young people can grow, heal, and build confidence. Our home is built on structure, compassion, and genuine care — giving youth the stability they need to rise above challenges and thrive in their future.
Rise & Thrive is committed to creating a warm, welcoming environment that supports emotional well‑being, personal development, and positive life skills. Our approach centers on respect, safety, and empowerment, ensuring every youth feels valued and supported throughout their journey.
Rise & Thrive is dedicated to partnering with families, agencies, and professionals who share our passion for helping young people succeed.

2. Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide a safe, stable, and nurturing home where youth ages 10–17 can grow, heal, and build confidence. Through compassion, consistency, and respect, we guide youth toward emotional well‑being, personal development, and long‑term success.

4. Expanded Program Description

Rise & Thrive Support Services, LLC is a residential group home dedicated to providing safe, structured, and compassionate care for youth ages 10–17. Our program is designed to support young people who need stability, guidance, and emotional support in a home‑like environment that promotes healing and personal growth.
We integrate trauma‑informed care, positive youth development, and individualized support to help each resident build confidence, develop life skills, and achieve meaningful progress. Daily programming includes emotional support, academic encouragement, healthy social interaction, and skill‑building activities that prepare youth for long‑term success.
